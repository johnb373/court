# Court Rotation

A simple tool for running social doubles sessions at a tennis club.
Built for Termonfeckin Social Tennis.

**Use it here: [johnb373.github.io/court](https://johnb373.github.io/court)**

It works on any phone. Open the link and use it straight away, or add it to
your home screen so it opens like a normal app.

### Adding it to your home screen

**Android (Chrome)**
1. Open the link in Chrome
2. Tap the **⋮** menu, top right
3. Tap **Install** (on older versions, **Add to Home screen**)
4. Give it a name and tap **Install**

If the menu offers both *Install* and *Create shortcut*, choose **Install** —
that gives you a proper app that opens full screen without the address bar.

**iPhone / iPad (Safari)**
1. Open the link in **Safari** — this doesn't work in Chrome on iPhone
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow)
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**
4. Give it a name and tap **Add**

Either way you end up with a tennis ball icon alongside your other apps.

## What it does

Enter the names of whoever turns up, tap which courts are free, and it builds
a round-by-round schedule showing who plays with whom and on which court.

- Up to 12 players, on up to 4 courts
- Sit-outs are shared out evenly — nobody sits out twice while someone else
  never sits at all, and nobody sits out two rounds running
- Partners change every round, with no repeat pairings
- Players can be added or removed mid-session; rounds already played stay put,
  and the rest are rebuilt around the change
- Extra rounds can be added at any point if there's time for another

## Two ways to run a session

**Rounds** (the default) — pick how many rounds you want and play at your own
pace. No timer, no countdown. Move on when the games finish.

**Timed** — set the time you need to be off court and it works out how many
rounds fit, then shows a running clock. Round boundaries are fixed to the
clock, so changeovers don't push the finish time back.

## Works without a signal

Open it once with a connection and the app stores itself on your phone. After
that it opens instantly whether you have signal or not. When you are online it
quietly checks for updates in the background, so it stays current without you
doing anything.

## Things worth knowing

- The schedule is saved on the phone. Close the app, or restart the phone,
  and reopening offers to resume the session where you left off.
- The **Full schedule** view shows every round on one screen. Screenshot it —
  that works even if the phone dies or there's no signal.
- Nothing is sent anywhere. Names and schedules stay on the phone that
  created them.

## Fairness

The rule the app never breaks: the difference between the player who sits out
most and the player who sits out least is never more than one round. Where the
numbers don't divide evenly, it tells you the split before you start, so you
can add a round if you'd rather even it up.

Ticking **shuffle** randomises the order before building, so the first name
typed isn't always the first to sit out.
